Working here

We look for people who are curious, careful and committed to doing excellent work for clients.

At {{firm.name}}, junior lawyers work directly with partners on live matters, receive regular feedback and are given responsibility as soon as they are ready for it. We value good judgement, clear writing and a collaborative approach as much as technical knowledge.

Opportunities

Advocates

We welcome applications from advocates of the High Court of Kenya with experience in any of our practice areas.

Business services

Our legal assistants, secretaries, accounts and administrative colleagues keep the firm running. We are happy to hear from experienced professionals.

Pupillage and attachments

Subject to availability, we consider applications for pupillage from Kenya School of Law students and for short attachments from university law students. Pupils and attachees work across practice areas, attend court and client meetings, and draft under supervision.
