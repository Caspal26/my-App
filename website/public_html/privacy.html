Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

OMC Advocates respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle personal data in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

OMC Advocates is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and in the course of our practice. You can contact us at info@omcadvocates.co.ke or by post at [Building name, floor]
[Street or road]
[Town / city], Kenya
[P.O. Box and postal code].

What we collect

  • Enquiries: the name, email address, telephone number and message you send us through the contact form, by email or by phone.
  • Clients and matters: information needed to advise and act for clients, including identification documents required for client due diligence.
  • Applicants: CVs and supporting information sent to us for recruitment.
  • Website use: standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) kept by our hosting provider for security and maintenance.

How we use it

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, check for conflicts of interest, provide legal services, meet our legal and regulatory obligations, consider job applications and keep our systems secure. We rely on your consent, the performance of a contract, our legal obligations or our legitimate interests, as applicable.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only where necessary, for example with courts, registries and other parties to a matter, our professional advisers, or service providers such as our hosting and email providers who process it on our instructions.

Retention and security

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for and to meet our professional and legal record-keeping obligations. We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to be informed about the use of your personal data, to access it, to object to its processing, and to have inaccurate or misleading data corrected or deleted. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@omcadvocates.co.ke. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them. If we add analytics or other cookies in future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where required.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
